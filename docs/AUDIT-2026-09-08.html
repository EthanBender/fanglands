The Walkable World

Can a ten-year-old actually walk this world end to end, reach everyone in it, and level every skill without hitting a wall? A bot played the whole story to find out.

The short version

  • The world is fully connected. Every named person, door, dungeon entrance, quest marker, boat landing and monster spawn can be walked to from the cave where the knight wakes. Nothing is stranded.
  • The whole story is completable by walking. A bot with no teleports played stage 0 to stage 16 in 40 minutes of game time, killed 100 monsters, died zero times, and slew The Fang.
  • Progression was the broken part, not the map. Farming needed 7,491 hours to master. Defence needed 1,328. Agility had 29 levels with nothing new to do. All three are fixed and the numbers are in this report.
  • One real navigation bug. The map marker for the Ashfields chapters pointed at a tile four squares off Dunstan's hut, so the compass aimed at empty ash.

How the world was checked

The audit floods the freshly generated map with a breadth-first search from every place the game actually puts the player: the cave where you wake, the ladder down to Deepholm, and each of the boat landings. A target counts as reached when the search arrives at it or at a tile beside it, because doors, signposts, boards and shrines are solid things you stand next to. Story gates are honoured: a target behind the warden's gate is only reachable once the stage that opens it has passed.

The bot played the whole game

Behind a flag (node tools/headless.js index.html 1 --play) the self-test stops checking and starts playing. It walks with the movement keys, fights with the swing key, talks by facing people, and crafts by clicking the real panel buttons. It is allowed to teleport only where the game itself teleports you: into a dungeon, or onto Harl's ferry.

Progression: what was broken

For every level the game asks for anywhere, the audit finds the best experience source available below that level and computes, at the code's own rates, how long the level takes. Four skills were far outside the pack, and two had long stretches where nothing new opened up. All of the fixes below are new things to do in the world. None of them multiplies an existing number.

What was fixed

Navigation The Ashfields map marker pointed at empty ash
Chapters 11 to 13 aimed the compass and the world map at tile 66,100. Dunstan's hut is at 67,104. A child following the arrow arrived at nothing, in the one region where wandering means dragons. Fixed in src/13-ux.js, both the stage markers and the side-quest marker.
Progression Farming could not be levelled: one crop for the whole game
Potatoes were the only thing that could be planted, at 1,740 experience an hour, which is 7,491 hours to master the skill. Three new crops now grow on the same soil and need a real Farming level to plant: herbs at 15, golden wheat at 35, dragonfruit at 55. Greta sells the seeds. Herbs make poultices, golden wheat makes a loaf that heals 24, dragonfruit heals 22 on its own.
Progression Defence had one source, and it was standing still and being hit
The only way to raise Defence was to take damage. A knight who fights well levelled it slowest. A hard kill now teaches Defence: any monster of level 10 or above pays its level times four, the same shape as the existing Melee bonus. The level-up announcement is deliberately kept out of the way of a boss banner, so killing the Barrelbeast still says what it should.
Progression Nothing to fish between level 5 and level 25, nothing to cook but trout
Pike arrive at Fishing 12 and salmon at 20, both in the river the world blend cut from Miller's Pond to the sea. Swordfish at 40 and shark at 60 live in the deep sea. Cooking now pays what the fish is worth instead of a flat 30 for everything, which is why mastering Cooking fell from 145 hours to 14.
Progression Agility had 29 levels with nothing new, and no shortcuts
Between the Thistledown yard course at level 1 and the Grey Quarry cliffs at 30, nothing opened. Three crossings now span the river at Agility 12, 22 and 35, each marked by a post on both banks. They are real shortcuts across a river that otherwise has to be walked around. No water tile was touched, so the world blend's river, its bridges and its checks are unchanged.
Progression Smithing ended at mithril
Past the mithril warhammer there was nothing repeatable to forge, leaving 876 hours to master. An obsidian helm at Smithing 42 and an obsidian blade at 45 now use the obsidian already mined in the Ashfields. Mastering Smithing fell to 97 hours.
Correctness A full pack silently ate a catch and its experience
Found while adding the new fish. The core drops a fish at your feet when the pack is full and still pays the experience; the first version of the new catch code refused it instead. It now matches the core.

Found, not fixed

Balance Sixty ash drakes for five dragon scales
The bot's own log: it killed 60 drakes to collect the five scales the Song of Above asks for, because the scale sits on a rare table. This is the single sharpest grind in the game. The fix is either a guaranteed scale from a named drake, or raising the drop rate for the quest's five and leaving it rare afterwards.
Progression Defence, Farming and Crafting still sit above the pack
After the fixes they are 603, 476 and 422 hours to master, against 41 to 181 for everything else. They are now finishable rather than impossible, but a further pass wants a higher-tier repeatable source in each: a shield-block source for Defence, a fourth crop above dragonfruit, and a high-level crafting item that consumes something plentiful.
Balance Melee has nothing new between 60 and The Fang at 80
Ash drakes at 780 experience a kill carry the player the whole way. It works, but the last twenty levels have no new monster to meet. A single new creature in the Ashfields or the Afterlands at level 65 to 70 would close it.
Duplication Two ways to forge Godly Plated, at different levels
Master Halcyon forges the set in Aerie at Smithing 30; the anvil recipes ask 32 to 40. This is deliberate as a fallback for a player who has not found the sky city, but the two paths should probably agree on a number.

What to build next

  1. Make the five quest dragon scales reachable without sixty kills.
  2. A level 65 to 70 monster, so Melee's last stretch has a new face in it.
  3. A shield-block source for Defence, so armour and defence reinforce each other.
  4. A fourth crop and one high-level crafting sink, closing the last two outliers.
  5. Agility courses in the jungle and on Grubmarket's roofs, for the 9 levels still empty.
  6. Reconcile the two Godly Plated smithing levels.
  7. Run --play in continuous integration, so a stranded person or a broken stage is caught the day it lands.
  8. Extend the playthrough past the credits, into the Afterlands and the Dragon Killers ending.
  9. Teach the bot to buy from shops, so the economy gets the same treatment as the map.
  10. A second bot run with kid mode on, to check the easier path is also completable.
