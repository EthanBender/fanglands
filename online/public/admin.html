Shared world

The world server will run the monsters itself, a step at a time (below: three small places so far, each off until you turn it on). This counts what Cloudflare's free plan counts: each call to the world is one request, and every 20 messages from the knights' games are one more. The free plan allows 100,000 a day, and its day starts at midnight UTC (8 pm here in summer, 7 pm in winter). This page's own calls (and the backups') are counted on their own, so the game's share is what the cost gate reads. The meter is read when this page opens and when you press Refresh; while the page is hidden, nothing refreshes at all.

Movement check

The movement check watches where each knight's game says he is, against the world's map: too fast for what he rides, or into or through a wall nobody can cross. It only counts; it never moves or stops anyone. A knight whose page is older than the world is asked to reload, and is not checked until he does.

Monsters run by the world

Today one knight's game runs the monsters of each place and the others watch it. Turned on here, the world runs that place's monsters itself, so everyone there sees the same ones. If anything goes wrong it gives the place back to a knight's game by itself, says why below, and leaves it that way until you turn it on again. Nobody's knight, pack or save is touched either way.
